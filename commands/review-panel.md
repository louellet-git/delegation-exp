Run a full review panel over:

$ARGUMENTS

1. **Panel:** [skeptical-review](../skills/skeptical-review/SKILL.md#panel-setup)
   over the whole integrated tree, with a `doc-standard` skeptic for any doc.
2. **NOT PLEASED:**
   [consolidating-review](../skills/consolidating-review/SKILL.md), through
   the fluff gate, one fix round, the dissenters re-reviewed.
3. **All PLEASED:** write
   [the artifact](../skills/skeptical-review/SKILL.md#the-review-artifact-feeds-the-commit-gate),
   [inspect](../skills/delegating/SKILL.md#manager-inspects-the-procedure),
   make one commit for the batch.
